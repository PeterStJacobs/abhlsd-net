Mars-Venus Separation

Angular separation and planet-to-planet distance

Local calculations via astronomy-engine. Toggle topocentric angle, scan across time, plot the paths, and switch the time-axis display between observer timezone and UTC.

Geocentric angle: from Earth’s centre. Same for all locations.

Topocentric angle: from your observing site. Includes parallax, so location can matter slightly.

  • For planets, the difference is usually small.
  • Plots, scan results, and CSV use whichever mode is selected.

Angle uses geocentric or topocentric mode depending on the checkbox. Distance is heliocentric. Alt/Az readouts are topocentric.
